About

To build or not to build.

In 2023 I stumbled across ChatGPT and knew it would change how people work. I took a planned sabbatical and dove into AI and automation. I wanted to apply what I learned to real businesses, with real customers and real costs, to see what the tools could do and separate hype from reality. So I built fully automated content pipelines, automated Shopify stores, AI-assisted newsletters, mobile apps and agents.

Most of those projects are retired, and a few stay up as demos. Each one taught me something new: about technology, about business, and maybe most importantly, about myself. The lessons learned are on their own page.

Those lessons feed directly into my consulting work: Lumio Consulting, for non-profits and small teams, and Movemnt.AI, for mid-sized organizations looking for someone to own AI.

If something here looks interesting or useful, or if you want to talk about building, don't hesitate to reach out!
