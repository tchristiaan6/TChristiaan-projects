About

Ideas are a dime a dozen. Execution is the whole game.

I spent nearly two decades in enterprise IT as a solutions architect, working with everyone from small businesses to Fortune 100 companies. In 2023 I started building on my own: e-commerce stores, automated content pipelines, physical products, newsletters, mobile apps, AI agents and consulting.

Most of these projects are retired. Each one taught me something I couldn't have learned any other way: own the product, distribution beats product, and passion is not the same as credibility. The ones still running are the ones where those lessons finally stuck.

I keep this page as a running record. If something here is useful to you, or you want to talk about building things, I'm easy to find.
