About

To build or not to build.

In 2023 I stumbled across ChatGPT and knew it would change how people work. I took a planned sabbatical and dove into AI and automation, applying the tools to real projects, some of them small businesses, to learn what they could do and separate hype from reality. I built fully automated content pipelines, automated Shopify stores, AI-assisted newsletters, mobile apps and agents.

Most of these projects are retired and some I keep around as demos, but each one taught me something: about technology, about business, and maybe most importantly, about myself. The lessons learned are on their own page.

I keep this page as a running record. If something here looks interesting or useful, or if you want to talk about building, don't hesitate to reach out!
