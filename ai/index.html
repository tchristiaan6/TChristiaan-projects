GridDown

griddown.us ↗
Problem
In a long power outage there is no connectivity, so no maps and no way to ask a question.
Built
A React Native app with downloadable offline vector maps and a small Llama model that runs on the device. A retrieval layer sits over 40-plus field guides sourced from CDC, EPA, FEMA, USGS and military manuals, and every answer cites its source. Four months on the app, six on the trusted knowledge base.
