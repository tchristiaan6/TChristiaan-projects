Peatsy Puzzles.
Piece by piece.

AI-generated images on jigsaw puzzles. The first fully automated, one-person company.

1person
482images below

Peatsy started as a domain name. The original idea was a subscription service for everyday home goods, a play on "repeatable," and it came along right as Amazon launched Subscribe and Save. The concept never went anywhere, but the domain stuck around.

Peatsy Puzzles came out of two things happening at once. AI image generation was brand new, and I loved making images with it. Printify made it easy to put those images on physical products, puzzles included. Combining the two was the obvious move: generate the artwork, put it on puzzles, and automate every step in between. A business that ran itself, and a way to learn automation tooling at the same time.

I loved building it, but Peatsy Puzzles had a margins problem. A forty-five dollar puzzle netted a dollar fifty to two dollars after costs, and no amount of automation changes that math. It was never going to be sustainable. What it did give me was a working knowledge of Make.com and the low-code, no-code toolbox, and the fundamentals of automation I've used on every project since.
